# Changelog

Player-facing changes of each published release, newest first.

## 0.5.0 (2026-11-22)

### Initial release

First release of Starstone.

- Build a power network that runs beside redstone. A network is simply on or off: when any generator or powered input in it is on, every cable and device connected to it gets power.
- Power has no strength levels and never fades with distance, so even a long cable line needs no repeaters. A change spreads through the whole network within moments; very large networks can take a little longer.
- The Starstone Redstone Input turns any redstone signal into Starstone power, so levers, buttons, observers and redstone circuits can switch a network. The Starstone Redstone Output turns the network back into a full-strength redstone signal for pistons, doors, lamps and other redstone devices.
- Cables, bridges, lamps and redstone adapters attach to floors, walls and ceilings, and cables join up on their own as you place them. A line can wrap around the outer edge of a block, for example from its top down onto its side.
- Craft a Starstone Inner Corner from two cables and place it in the inside corner where a floor meets a wall, or where two walls meet. It joins the cable lines on both faces into one network.
- A Starstone Conduit is a solid block that carries power straight through from one end to the other, so you can pass power through a wall or floor. It points the way you place it, and its sides do not connect.
- A Starstone Bridge lets two cable lines cross in the same spot without connecting. Each line keeps its own power, so one can be on while the other is off.
- The Starstone Generator powers its network as soon as you place it; use it to switch it off and on. The Starstone Lamp gives full-brightness light while its network is powered.
- Starstone Ore lies underground in the Overworld and glows faintly. Mine it with an iron, diamond or netherite pickaxe for 2-4 Starstone Dust and some experience; Fortune gives more dust and Silk Touch keeps the ore block.
- Cables need only copper and Starstone Dust. Four dust and a copper ingot make Compressed Starstone Dust, which a furnace smelts into a Starstone Crystal for the generator, lamp and redstone adapters. Recipes unlock when you first pick up the material they use.
