> **Test build.** This is a pre-release for testing. Things may still change before the public release, so back up any world you care about. Feedback is welcome.

Starstone adds a glowing power network that works beside redstone: cables for floors, walls and ceilings, generators, lamps, and adapters to and from redstone.

It works on its own and needs none of the author's other add-ons.

Starstone Ore generates only in newly generated chunks, underground in the Overworld at Y 15 and below, so start a new world or explore areas you have not visited yet.
